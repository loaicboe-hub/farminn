# Optional Module: Industrial & Factories

Activate only for manufacturing facilities, industrial groups, factory brokerages, or industrial management platforms.

## Core Entities (Additive Tables When Activated)

- `industrial_categories` + `industrial_category_translations`: Sector classifications (metals, food processing, packaging, logistics, etc.).
- `factories` + `factory_translations`: Factory profile, facility area, production lines, certifications, manufacturing capabilities JSON, transaction/service mode (`buy` | `sell` | `rent` | `management` | `partnership`), gallery/spec PDFs.
- `industrial_inquiries`: Request category (`partnership`, `buy`, `sell`, `rent`, `management`, `oem_manufacturing`), company profile, contact info, status.

## Admin Capabilities

- Manage factory profiles, industrial categories, capabilities, and technical attachments.
- Separate public contact channels (governed centrally via Website Settings) from internal inquiry triage.
- Enforce strict upload security for engineering spec PDFs and facility images.
